## 2. Character Profiles

### Akshay (The Automation Hero • Protagonist)
• **Role**: Junior Automation & Software Engineer (~23 years old). Hungry to learn, quick to write code, deeply curious, and relatable to every beginner developer.
• **Visual Anchors**:
  • Clean shaven, modern short black hair with a slight lock on his forehead.
  • Sharp, expressive almond eyes rendered with authentic Madhubani double line black ink contours.
  • Signature Attire: Tailored mustard yellow (saffron) modern cotton kurta with rolled up sleeves, subtle geometric Madhubani border on the mandarin collar, dark charcoal trousers, and a black smartwatch on his left wrist.
• **Personality & Emotional Arc**:
  • Energetic and eager to test live APIs.
  • Makes authentic, realistic beginner mistakes: forgetting parentheses on `json()`, hardcoding IDs leading to unique constraint rejections, writing bare boolean tests without Chai matchers, and overlooking teardown cleanups.
  • Highly expressive reactions: scratches the back of his head in comic distress during unexpected 500 or 400 errors, and pumps his fist in genuine celebration during eureka moments.

### Sameer (The Principal Architect • The Mentor)
• **Role**: Seasoned Principal Architect and Enterprise Reliability Veteran (~40 years old). Battle tested from a decade of production outages, calm, patient, and deeply grounded in first principles.
• **Visual Anchors**:
  • Neatly trimmed salt and pepper beard, thin wireframe spectacles, distinguished and calm almond eyes.
  • Signature Attire: Deep indigo blue linen kurta under an off white (beige) Nehru waistcoat (bandi jacket) with subtle traditional Madhubani pocket embroidery, dark charcoal trousers, and leather mojari shoes.
• **Personality & Mentorship Style**:
  • Never scolds or lectures; teaches through first principles and relatable production war stories ("gyan").
  • Frequently holds a steaming terracotta tea cup (kulhad) or brass tumbler of cutting chai.
  • Gestures with classical teaching mudras (open explanatory palm, pointing with a stylus) to demystify TCP wire packets, memory heaps, and race conditions.
  • Smiles knowingly when Akshay hits a classic interview or production trap, then guides him to build resilient, self healing automation.

## 3. Visual & Aesthetic Rules for Generation

1. **Art Style**:
   • Authentic Indian Madhubani (Mithila) folk art graphic novel style.
   • Distinct double line black ink contours, sharp almond eyes, and rich flat color fills.
   • Strictly no photorealistic humans.
2. **Environment (Indic Modernity)**:
   • State of the art engineering laboratory integrated into contemporary sandstone architecture.
   • Carved Nagara stone jali screens, Dravidian pillared alcoves, teakwood standing desks, dual 4K monitors, mechanical keyboards, and glass whiteboards.
   • Clean light mode palette on pure white or sun bleached ivory backgrounds (`#FFFFFF` / `#F8FAFC`).
3. **Dialogue Clouds**:
   • Rounded comic speech clouds with black outlines.
   • Cloud pointers direct attention to the technical action: pointing directly to code lines, JSON keys, or wire packets.
   • English only inside all visual artwork. Strictly no Hindi or Devanagari script.
4. **Code & Screen Integration**:
   • Paired with dedicated SVG / CSS components for the Code IDE and API Testing Workbench to ensure 100% pixel perfect typography and readability.
