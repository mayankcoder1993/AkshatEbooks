- **Server Alcoves:** Matte-black 42U enterprise server racks recessed directly inside ancient vaulted sandstone alcoves, their perforated mesh doors glowing with rows of pulsing electric-cyan, emerald, and amber fiber-optic activity LEDs.
- **Pair-Programming Workbenches:** Heavy, 100-year-old solid carved Burma teak tables fitted with minimalist matte-black dual-monitor gas-strut arms holding ultra-wide curved 4K OLED bezel-less displays.
- **Laptops & Peripherals:** Sleek unibody anodized aluminum laptops, custom low-profile mechanical keyboards with warm amber underglow, braided black and cyan data cables routed neatly through machined brass grommets into discrete floor channels.
- **Diagnostics & Network Gear:** High-precision digital network packet analyzers, portable gigabit fiber switches, and Sameer’s slim matte-black diagnostic slate tablet resting alongside traditional steaming cutting chai glasses in ornate brass wire holders.
- **NO Magical Sci-Fi:** No floating glowing crystals or magical rivers. Real physical computers, real copper/fiber wires, real teak tables, real clay kulhars, and real brass dinner thalis.

### 3. Framing & Dialogue Ceiling Invariant:
- **Aspect Ratio:** Strict `16:9` widescreen format (`1408x768` or `1365x768`).
- **Full Bleed Borderless:** Edge-to-edge artwork. No picture frames, no margins, no decorative borders.
- **Dialogue Ceiling:** Top 25% to 30% of every composition must remain clean negative space (sandstone arches, vaulted ceiling, or morning/evening sky) to accommodate dynamic SVG/CSS speech balloons.
