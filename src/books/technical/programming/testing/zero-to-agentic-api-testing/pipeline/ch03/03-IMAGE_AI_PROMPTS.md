## 0. AGENT MISSION BRIEF & IMAGE AUDIT PROTOCOL (READ FIRST)

> 🛑 **MANDATORY PRE-GENERATION AUDIT CRITERIA:**
> 1. **Character Hard Locks:**
>    - **Akshay (Apprentice Engineer):** 24-year-old North Indian male, lean athletic build, dark wavy hair. **Clean, natural forehead with ZERO tilak or sectarian markings**. Crisp **white handloom cotton kurta** with sleeves rolled up to mid-forearm, blue denim trousers. Working on a matte-silver laptop with a **distinct horizontal silver scratch across the top-left lid edge**. High-energy, intense analytical concentration.
>    - **Sameer Krishnamurthy (Principal Systems Architect):** 40-year-old South Indian male, serene poised presence, neatly trimmed salt-and-pepper beard, silver streaks at temples, thin round brass wireframe spectacles. Wearing a **peacock-indigo raw-silk kurta** with gold mandarin collar embroidery, cream trousers. Always holding or standing beside a **traditional faceted cutting chai glass in an ornate raw brass wire holder**. Hands never touch a junior's keyboard.
> 2. **Environment Hard Lock:** Solid **300-year-old carved Indian red and beige sandstone architecture** integrated seamlessly with **modern high-throughput hardware** (matte-black server consoles, curved OLED displays, brass task lamps).
> 3. **Screen & Document Authenticity (NO FOLK-ART IN TECHNOLOGY):**
>    - Screens, terminals, laptops, and printed documents must look like **authentic, sharp, photorealistic developer equipment**.
>    - Workbench interfaces show **real HTTP status badges (200 OK in emerald `#10B981`, 401 Unauthorized in amber `#F59E0B`, 500 Crash in red `#E53935`)**, real JSON payloads, and real Chai BDD assertions (`pm.test`, `pm.expect`).
>    - Absolutely NO decorative tribal patterns, folk squiggles, or pretend runes on screens or papers!
> 4. **Framing & Dialogue Ceiling:**
>    - **Strictly 16:9 full-bleed borderless.** No outer borders, no floral borders, no photo frames.
>    - **Dialogue Ceiling:** Keep the **top 25% to 30% of the canvas clean negative space** (stone arches, vaulted ceiling, ambient lamp pool, or sky) to allow dynamic SVG speech balloons.
>    - **NO baked-in speech bubbles or floating text in the raster artwork.** All dialogue lives in SVG overlays.
