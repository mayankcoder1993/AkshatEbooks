## KEY DECISIONS MADE

DECISION 1:
  What was decided: The 13 chapters are mapped into three distinct missions with clear stakes: Mission 1 Reading the Wire (Chapters 1 to 3), Mission 2 Building the Watchdog (Chapters 4 to 8), and Mission 3 Guarding the Gate (Chapters 9 to 13).
  Why this option was chosen: Topic labels like Fundamentals or Intermediate do not provide dramatic tension or clear emotional milestones for adult career changers.
  Options that were rejected: Generic 13 chapter syllabus without mission framing was rejected because learner motivation drops sharply between Chapters 4 and 6 without narrative progression.
  Impact on subsequent stages: Every storyboard must declare its mission and respect its corresponding mission tone envelope.

DECISION 2:
  What was decided: Akshay represents the QA Manual tester seeking automation competence, paired with Sameer as Senior Mentor in a Madhubani Pan Indian heritage aesthetic.
  Why this option was chosen: Grounding technical concepts in relatable workplace mentorship and cultural warmth eliminates clinical textbook dryness.
  Options that were rejected: Corporate glass office setting with photorealistic avatars was rejected per core platform aesthetics.
  Impact on subsequent stages: All storyboards and illustration prompts must enforce light mode backgrounds, Dravidian pillars, teak furniture, and Madhubani almond eye character styling.

DECISION 3:
  What was decided: Software workbenches must be rendered via interactive SVG and code markup rather than AI generated bitmap illustrations.
  Why this option was chosen: Software interfaces generated by image models suffer from distorted text, unselectable tokens, and hallucinated buttons.
  Options that were rejected: Generating all UI screens with image models.
  Impact on subsequent stages: Storyboards must distinguish between narrative scene panels and technical workbench screens.
