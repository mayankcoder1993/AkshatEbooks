  Never sign a paper, click a link, or accept a condition that you cannot
  explain to your grandmother in two plain sentences. Common sense is the
  highest form of legal and financial armor.

  Translates confusing bank notifications, digital apps, and government schemes
  into everyday kitchen and household metaphors. Builds confidence through
  safe practice, removing the fear of modern technology.

principled_refusal:
  Will never tolerate an expert, bureaucrat, or salesperson intimidating an ordinary
  citizen with high sounding English jargon. She immediately interrupts: "Speak in
  words that my vegetable vendor can understand, or do not speak at all."

## SECTION C: VOICE AND PERSONALITY SPEC

vocabulary_tendencies:
  Favours: warm maternal idioms, grounding household terms.
    Says "beta", "family savings", "hard earned money", "lock and key",
    "receipt in your hand", "never rush in haste".
  Avoids: cold corporate jargon (replaces "two factor authentication" with
    "the double lock on the front door").
  Characteristic phrase pattern: "Beta, let me tell you what this really means for your household."

sentence_patterns:
  Conversational, rhythmic, interspersed with rhetorical questions and homely wisdom.

humour_style:
  Warm, earthy grandmotherly wit that deflates pompous authority.
  Example: "That bank manager talks as if he invented money; remember, beta,
    he is just the watchman of the vault you filled with your sweat."

response_to_hero_mistake:
  Offers a glass of cool water: "Do not worry; no one is born knowing how to use
  a magic glass phone. Drink this water, take a breath, and we will do it together safely."

response_to_hero_success:
  "Look at that! You spotted the fake link all by yourself!
  Now nobody in this world can steal your pension money."

authentic_dialogue_examples:
  1. "Your bank will never call you asking for a secret number.
     If the Prime Minister himself calls asking for an OTP, hang up the phone!"
  2. "A hospital bill is not a holy scripture; ask for the itemized pharmacy list
     before your son touches his fixed deposit."
  3. "If someone promises to double your money in six months, hold your purse tightly
     and walk swiftly in the opposite direction."
  4. "You worked thirty years for this pension. You do not owe an apology to a bank clerk
     for asking where your interest went."
  5. "Technology is like a sharp kitchen knife: in wise hands it chops vegetables for dinner;
     in reckless hands it cuts your fingers. We will teach your hands to be wise."

wrong_dialogue_examples:
  1. "Old people can never learn how to use smartphones properly."
  2. "Just click on the link and see what happens; what is the worst that could happen?"
  3. "You do not need to read the contract terms; just sign where they marked an X."
  4. "The bank is an authority; they can never make a mistake."
  5. "Let us use complex financial derivatives to hedge the household risk."
