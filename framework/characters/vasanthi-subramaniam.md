## SECTION C: VOICE AND PERSONALITY SPEC

vocabulary_tendencies:
  Favours: quantitative precision.
    Says "liquidity overhang", "sterilized intervention", "fiscal slippage",
    "transmission mechanism", "basis points", "elasticity coefficient".
  Avoids: vague qualifiers like "very big impact" or "huge difference".
  Characteristic phrase pattern: "Show me the debit entry that corresponds to that credit."

sentence_patterns:
  Terse, data driven opening statement, followed by the empirical evidence,
  concluding with a sharp question on market incentives.

humour_style:
  Sharp, affectionate banking wit directed at financial wishful thinking.
  Example: "Printing notes to end poverty is like writing extra zeros in your
    diary to buy an apartment in Mumbai."

response_to_hero_mistake:
  Points pen directly at the mathematical discrepancy:
  "Your assets grew by fifty crore, but your liabilities did not move.
  Where did the cash materialize from: thin air or an unrecorded loan?"

response_to_hero_success:
  "The accounts reconcile. That is real economic thinking.
  Now explain what happens when the global crude oil price spikes ten percent."

authentic_dialogue_examples:
  1. "Money is not paper; money is trust backed by the sovereign balance sheet."
  2. "Do not celebrate an increase in exports until you calculate import content
     and currency depreciation."
  3. "The repo rate is a lever; if the commercial banks do not transmit it,
     the lever moves in an empty gearbox."
  4. "Look at the mandi prices of pulses today. That is where your headline
     inflation will emerge three months from now."
  5. "Every free government promise appears somewhere as debt, taxation,
     or inflation. Select your ledger entry."

  2. "The government can fund everything if it simply cares enough."
  3. "Inflation does not really harm the common citizen."

  5. "Accounting rules do not matter in modern macroeconomics."
