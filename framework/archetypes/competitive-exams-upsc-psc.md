# Pedagogical Archetype: Competitive Examinations (UPSC & State PSC)

This archetype governs comprehensive preparation manuals for high stakes competitive civil services examinations (UPSC CSE, State PSCs such as MPPSC, UPPSC, BPSC, RAS, KPSC).

---

## 1. Core Narrative Vehicle: The Analytical Policy War Room

Civil service aspirants are not training to be rote memorizers; they are future district administrators and policy makers. Content is framed as **District Collector Case Studies and War Room Briefings**:
• **The Senior Bureaucrat / Mentor:** Guides the aspirant through complex governance challenges, land disputes, crisis management, and policy trade offs.
• **The Aspirant Investigator:** Analyzes historical trends, compares constitutional safeguards, evaluates socio economic data, and drafts actionable administrative interventions.

---

## 2. Mandatory Pedagogical Blocks

1. **Official Syllabus Mapping Badge:**
   • Explicit linkage to exam papers (e.g. `GS PAPER 2 · GOVERNANCE, CONSTITUTION & SOCIAL JUSTICE`).
   • Direct syllabus topic citation.
2. **Verified Past-Year Questions (PYQ) Cards:**
   • Official metadata: Exam, Year, Paper, and Marks (e.g. `UPSC CSE Mains 2021 · GS Paper 2 · 15 Marks · 250 Words`).
   • Official primary source URL to the UPSC/PSC official paper repository.
   • Multi dimensional Model Answer: Introduction (definition/statutory basis), Body (analytical arguments, constitutional articles, committee reports), and Forward Looking Conclusion.
   • Official Marking Scheme Criteria: What earns top percentile marks vs what yields average score deductions.
3. **High-Yield Prelims Trap Matrix:**
   • Common UPSC question traps: extreme statements (*all*, *none*, *only*), inverted timeline chronology, confusing constitutional bodies with statutory bodies.
4. **Administrative Case Study:**
   • Ethical dilemmas in district administration, conflicting stakeholder interests, and balanced legal resolutions.
