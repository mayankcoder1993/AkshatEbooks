# Pedagogical Archetype: Multidisciplinary & Hybrid Subjects (e.g. GST)

Many transformative subjects cannot be confined to a single academic silo. Landmark topics like the **Goods and Services Tax (GST)**, Land Reforms, Environmental Jurisprudence, and FinTech require a seamless synthesis across **Constitutional Law**, **Fiscal Economics**, and **Historical Evolution**.

---

## 1. The Three-Pillar Narrative Matrix: The Case of GST

When teaching a multifaceted subject like GST, the curriculum alternates across three distinct lenses:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ PILLAR 1: The Historical Origin Lens                                        │
│           • The pre GST cascading tax maze (CENVAT, Octroi, VAT, CST)       │
│           • The decades long political consensus journey (Kelkar Committee) │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ PILLAR 2: The Constitutional Law Lens                                       │
│           • The 101st Constitutional Amendment Act                          │
│           • Article 246A (Simultaneous power to tax)                        │
│           • Article 279A (The GST Council: federal voting consensus)       │
│           • Supreme Court landmark precedent (Mohit Minerals case)          │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ PILLAR 3: The Economic & Commercial Mechanics Lens                          │
│           • Input Tax Credit (ITC) ledger mechanics and invoice matching    │
│           • Anti profiteering provisions and consumer price index impact    │
│           • Inter state trade mechanics: CGST, SGST, and IGST pool sharing  │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

---

## 2. Mandatory Hybrid Pedagogical Blocks

1. **The Multidisciplinary Triad Badge:** Every chapter opener indicates the dominant lens of each section: `HISTORICAL CONTEXT`, `CONSTITUTIONAL LAW`, or `ECONOMIC MECHANICS`.
2. **The Cascading Tax vs Input Tax Credit Visual Comparison:**
   • Side by side invoice flow tracing raw materials $\rightarrow$ manufacturer $\rightarrow$ distributor $\rightarrow$ retailer $\rightarrow$ consumer.
   • Step by step calculation proving how eliminating tax on tax lowers final retail shelf prices.
3. **The GST Council Chamber Debate:**
   • Dialogue between Union Finance Minister and State Finance Ministers weighing state revenue protection versus national economic integration.
4. **Interactive Commercial Ledger Simulator:**
   • Step by step accounting cards showing GSTR 1 outward supplies reconciling with GSTR 2B automatic draft statements.
