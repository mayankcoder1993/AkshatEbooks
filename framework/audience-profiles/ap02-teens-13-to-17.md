# Audience Profile: Teens (13 to 17 Years)

Profile ID: ap02
Target Age: 13 to 17 Years
Cognitive Tier: Formal Operational / Abstract Identity Formation
Reading Level: High School

---

## 1. Demographic and Psychological Profile

Target Reader:
  High school students preparing for competitive entrance tests (JEE, NEET, CUET),
  exploring coding, robotics, creative arts, or foundational civic sciences.

Attention Span:
  20 to 25 minutes per session.
  Habituated to fast digital content, mobile feeds, and interactive video games.
  Possesses low tolerance for condescending adult instruction or archaic textbook language.

Core Motivation:
  Gaining real mastery, building cool projects to show friends, discovering personal identity,
  achieving competitive advantage in school, and feeling competent and autonomous.

Frustration Triggers:
  Patronizing or overly childish examples that insult their intelligence.
  Dry rote memorization formulas presented without underlying logic or real-world relevance.
  Rhetorical preachy moralizing or corporate jargon.

---

## 2. Formatting and Calibration Rules

Tone and Voice:
  Direct, witty, culturally current, respectful, and peer-collaborative.
  Treats the reader as a budding creator, builder, or investigator.
  Uses smart humor, gaming metaphors, and pop culture parallels without cringey slang.

Vocabulary Constraints:
  Modern standard vocabulary.
  Technical terms introduced directly with precise definitions, paired with vivid mechanical models.
  Complex terminology is respected, not diluted, but always decoded immediately.

Sentence Length Limits:
  Maximum 16 words per sentence in dialogue.
  Maximum 18 words per sentence in narrative exposition boxes.

Prose Budget per Scene:
  15 to 35 words per panel.
  Visuals demonstrate the cause and effect mechanics, diagrams, and code snippets.

Visual Density and Panel Layout:
  3 to 5 panels per page.
  Dynamic comic layouts with diagonal panel borders during high-stakes sequences.
  Integration of modern app interfaces, game HUDs, and sleek schematic blueprints.

Gameplay and Interactivity:
  Moderate to high interactivity.
  Level up progress bars, diagnostic branch choices, Easter egg secrets, and code tweak puzzles.
  Boss battles at the climax of each mission unit.

---

## 3. Chapter Special Rules

Opening Hook:
  Must open with a relatable high-stakes problem or viral scenario
  (for example, a broken game server, a hacked social account, or a tricky physics paradox).

Closing Rule:
  Every chapter must conclude with a "Weekend Hack Project" or "Cheat Sheet Blueprint"
  that allows the student to build, run, or prove something tangible within 30 minutes.
