# Audience Profile: General Adults and Homemakers

Profile ID: ap05
Target Age: 25 to 55 Years
Cognitive Tier: Applied Practical / Lifelong Learning
Reading Level: General Audience / Everyday Civic Utility

---

## 1. Demographic and Psychological Profile

Target Reader:
  Everyday citizens, homemakers, small business owners, and non-specialist adults seeking
  to understand digital banking, personal finance, consumer rights, constitutional basics,
  health literacy, or everyday technology.

Attention Span:
  15 to 25 minutes per reading window, often interspersed between household tasks,
  family responsibilities, or business operations.

Core Motivation:
  Financial self-reliance, protecting family savings from digital scams, understanding
  household economics and taxation, asserting consumer rights, and demystifying modern technology.

Frustration Triggers:
  Intimidating academic jargon, dense mathematical equations without practical relevance,
  condescending technical assumptions, and overly complex administrative steps.

---

## 2. Formatting and Calibration Rules

Tone and Voice:
  Warm, respectful, empowering, relatable, and reassuring.
  A trusted neighborhood friend, family advisor, or community elder explaining how things work.
  De-stigmatizes hesitation around technology or financial systems.

Vocabulary Constraints:
  Simple, clear, and colloquial everyday language.
  Jargon is avoided; where unavoidable (such as OTP, UPI PIN, KYC, interest compounding),
  it is explained with simple domestic analogies (locks, keys, ledgers, identity cards).

Sentence Length Limits:
  Maximum 14 words per sentence in dialogue.
  Maximum 16 words per sentence in narrative exposition boxes.

Prose Budget per Scene:
  15 to 30 words per panel.
  Clear step-by-step visual illustrations showing real phone screens, bank statements,
  and official forms.

Visual Density and Panel Layout:
  3 to 4 panels per page.
  Bright, clear visual compositions with familiar household and neighborhood settings.
  Clear magnifying-glass callouts highlighting important details (such as warning signs on phishing messages).

Gameplay and Interactivity:
  Practical situational quizzes.
  "Spot the Scam" exercises, household budget balancing games, and checklist verifications.

---

## 3. Chapter Special Rules

Opening Hook:
  Must open with a common real-world domestic situation
  (for example, an unexpected bank SMS, a confusing utility bill, or an online purchase question).

Closing Rule:
  Every chapter must conclude with an "Action Checklist for the Home"
  (such as a 3-step security check for smartphones, or a simple household budget template)
  that can be implemented within 10 minutes.
