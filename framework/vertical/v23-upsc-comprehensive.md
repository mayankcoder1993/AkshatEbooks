## PART A: SUBJECT INVARIANTS

### A1: What Must Be Taught

FOUNDATIONAL LAYER:
  Syllabus deconstruction: micro-thematic mapping of Preliminary GS-1,
    Mains GS Papers 1 to 4, and Essay requirements.
  PYQ taxonomy: structural analysis of last 10 years of UPSC questions
    identifying recurring themes, trap mechanisms, and evolving depth.
  Core conceptual clarity: zero rote memorization; understanding underlying
    causes, historical context, constitutional roots, and economic drivers.
  Elimination techniques for Prelims: extreme statement traps, association errors,
    chronological inconsistencies, and probability-based risk calculation.

PRACTICAL LAYER (Mains Answer Architecture):
  Question demand decoding: dissecting directives (Discuss, Critically Analyze,
    Elucidate, Evaluate, Comment) and multi-part sub-questions.
  Answer structure: crisp introduction (definition/context/data), multi-dimensional
    body (PESTLE: Political, Economic, Social, Technological, Legal, Environmental),
    and forward-looking visionary conclusion (SDGs, constitutional ideals, committees).
  Value addition integration: constitutional articles, landmark Supreme Court cases,
    committee recommendations, international indices, and schematic diagrams.
  Time and space discipline: framing 150-word (10-mark) and 250-word (15-mark)
    answers within 7 and 11 minutes respectively.

PROFESSIONAL / INTEGRATED MASTERY LAYER:
  Inter-paper synthesis: connecting GS-1 geography with GS-3 agriculture,
    GS-2 governance with GS-4 ethical administrative dilemmas.
  Contemporary issue triangulation: static knowledge + dynamic current affairs +
    policy critique.
  Ethics and Case Studies (GS-4): stakeholder mapping, ethical dilemmas,
    administrative options evaluation, and justification using ethical frameworks.
  Personality test orientation: balanced worldview, administrative temperament,
    constitutional loyalty, and intellectual integrity.

### A2: Proof System

ALL questions, answer templates, and factual citations must satisfy:
  100% verified Previous Years' Questions (PYQs) with Year, Paper, and Question Number.
  Official UPSC answer key validation for Prelims questions where keys are published.
  Standard reference sources explicitly credited (NCERT, standard statutory texts,
    Economic Survey, Union Budget, NITI Aayog reports).
  Scoring rubrics broken down into concrete marks (Introduction 2m, Body Points 5m,
    Way Forward 2m, Visual/Schematic 1m).

### A3: Interactive Element Types

Mains Answer Blueprint Workbench (mains-answer.svg.js):
  Interactive mockup of UPSC answer booklet with ruled margins,
  word count indicators, header tags, schematic callouts, and point-form layout.

Prelims Question Dissector Workbench:
  Interactive 4-statement MCQ showing statement-by-statement elimination logic,
  highlighting extreme words ("all", "never", "only") and factual traps.

Stakeholder Matrix (GS-4 Ethics):
  Visual web showing all affected parties, conflicting duties, ethical principles,
  and long-term administrative consequences.

Syllabus-to-PYQ Heatmap:
  Visual thematic grid displaying examination frequency across the last 10 years
  for each micro-topic.

## PART B: CREATIVE VEHICLE PALETTE

VEHICLE 1: THE ACADEMY WAR ROOM
World: Civil service probationers at the Lal Bahadur Shastri National Academy
  of Administration (LBSNAA) undergo intensive policy simulation exercises,
  tackling real-world district governance, disaster response, and legal crises
  using their foundational preparation knowledge.

VEHICLE 2: THE DISTRICT CRISIS COUNCIL
World: An inter-departmental task force headed by a District Magistrate,
  Superintendent of Police, and Chief Development Officer resolves complex
  socio-economic unrest, drought relief, and developmental bottlenecks,
  mirroring GS-1, 2, 3, and 4 challenges.

VEHICLE 3: THE HIGH-POWERED COMMISSION INVESTIGATION
World: A national committee of inquiry examines a multifaceted systemic crisis
  (e.g. urban flood resilience, agrarian distress, or cybersecurity threats),
  synthesizing historical data, legal frameworks, and innovative policy roadmaps.
