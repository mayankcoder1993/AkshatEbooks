## PART A: SUBJECT INVARIANTS

### A1: What Must Be Taught

This module targets objective, multiple-choice, screening, and tier-1 examinations
(e.g. SSC CGL/CHSL, State PSC Prelims, Banking PO/Clerk, Railway RRB, CDS/NDA,
and Teacher Eligibility Tests) characterized by high question velocity,
negative marking, and factual precision.

FOUNDATIONAL LAYER:
  High-yield factual mapping: core facts, dates, constitutional amendments,
    scientific units, formulas, geographical landmarks, and statutory provisions.
  Pattern recognition: recurring question formats, common distractor options,
    and semantic traps.
  Speed and accuracy fundamentals: mental math shortcuts, structural scanning,
    and fast reading protocols.
  Risk and negative marking management: threshold calculation for attempting
    uncertain questions based on option elimination.

PRACTICAL LAYER:
  Mnemonic systems: phonetic peg systems, spatial memory palaces, acronyms,
    and associative visual mnemonics tailored to Indian context.
  Rapid elimination algorithms: 30-second elimination heuristics for 4-option MCQs.
  Flashcard and spaced repetition architecture: active recall triggers,
    leitner box schedules, and high-frequency revision loops.
  Speed-drills: structured timed test sprints (e.g. 20 questions in 10 minutes)
    with post-drill mistake ledgers.

EXAM MASTERY LAYER:
  Sectional time allocation: dynamic time-management strategies across
    Quantitative Aptitude, Reasoning, General Awareness, and English.
  Mistake classification ledger: categorizing errors into conceptual gaps,
    misreading questions, calculation blunders, or rushed guesses.
  Cut-off analytics: understanding score distributions, normalization formulas,
    and target safe-attempt percentiles.
  Last-mile revision protocol: hyper-condensed one-page cheat sheets and
    rapid formula sheets for the final 48 hours.

### A2: Proof System

ALL objective items and mnemonics must satisfy:
  100% verified question citations from official exam papers (with Year, Shift, and Tier).
  Arithmetic and reasoning shortcuts must include mathematical proof of validity
    under all boundary conditions (not just cherry-picked examples).
  Mnemonics must be tested for zero ambiguity and cross-concept collision.
  Accuracy stats and time benchmarks must be verified against actual timed trials.

### A3: Interactive Element Types

Rapid MCQ Sifter Workbench (mcq-sifter.svg.js):
  Interactive 4-choice screen with timer, visual strikethrough for eliminated distractors,
  and instant mnemonic clue toggle.

Memory Anchor Card:
  Standardized 4-part visual card:
    1. Raw Factual Data (Target)
    2. Vivid Indian Mnemonic / Visual Peg
    3. Structural Memory Hook
    4. Trap Distractor Warning

Speed-Math Ledger:
  Side-by-side comparison of conventional 2-minute textbook calculation
  versus 15-second Vedic/mental shortcut with verification proof.

Mistake Taxonomy Matrix:
  Color-coded feedback grid tracking user accuracy, time spent per question,
  and specific error type.
