## PART A: SUBJECT INVARIANTS

### A1: What Must Be Taught

FOUNDATIONAL LAYER:
  Corporate personality: separate legal entity, limited liability,
    Solomon v Solomon, and lifting the corporate veil.
  Incorporation and governance: Articles and Memorandum of Association,
    Director duties under the Companies Act, fiduciary obligations.
  Taxation foundations: direct vs indirect tax, taxable event,
    charge of income tax, residence rules, supply under GST.
  Compliance architecture: statutory audits, filing schedules,
    regulatory boards (MCA, SEBI, CBDT, CBIC).

PRACTICAL LAYER:
  Corporate transactions: board resolutions, shareholder approvals,
    related party transactions, and disclosure norms.
  Income tax computation: five heads of income, deductions,
    taxable income, and advance tax schedules.
  GST mechanics: input tax credit (ITC) matching, reverse charge
    mechanism (RCM), place of supply, and invoice generation.
  Tax planning vs tax evasion: Duke of Westminster vs McDowell
    and Vodafone doctrines, GAAR (General Anti Avoidance Rules).

PROFESSIONAL / EXAM MASTERY LAYER:
  Mergers and acquisitions: schemes of arrangement, NCLT process,
    due diligence checklists, Section 230 to 240 procedures.
  Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC): CIRP trigger, Committee of
    Creditors, resolution plan approval, liquidation waterfall.
  Cross-border tax: double taxation avoidance agreements (DTAA),
    transfer pricing, permanent establishment (PE) risks.
  Litigation and dispute resolution: ITAT, CESTAT, NCLAT procedures,
    settlement commissions, compounding of offenses.

### A2: Proof System

ALL tax calculations and statutory references must be exact:
  Applicable Assessment Year (AY) and Financial Year (FY) explicitly noted.
  Exact Section and Sub-section citations of Companies Act, Income Tax Act,
    CGST Act, and relevant circulars/notifications.
  Computation tables must demonstrate line-by-line arithmetic proof.
  Case precedents must cite official reporters (CTR, ITR, Comp Cas).

### A3: Interactive Element Types

Balance Sheet and Tax Computation Ledger:
  Interactive multi-column financial statements showing adjustments,
  disallowances, and net tax liabilities.

Corporate Entity Structure Diagram:
  Shows holding companies, subsidiaries, special purpose vehicles (SPVs),
  and flow of funds with regulatory checkpoints.

GST Invoicing and ITC Reconciliation Card:
  Visual invoice matching buyer GSTR-2B with seller GSTR-1,
  highlighting eligible vs ineligible ITC.

NCLT Courtroom / Boardroom Simulation:
  Visual interaction between directors, independent auditors,
  and insolvency professionals navigating compliance hurdles.

## PART B: CREATIVE VEHICLE PALETTE

VEHICLE 1: THE STARTUP DUE DILIGENCE AUDIT
World: A boutique corporate advisory firm must perform exhaustive
  tax and legal due diligence on a fast-growing tech unicorn prior
  to a multi-million-dollar acquisition, discovering hidden tax liabilities.

VEHICLE 2: THE GST COUNCIL ESCAPE ROOM
World: Tax consultants and state revenue officers model a complex
  multi-state supply chain across goods and services, untangling
  cross-border place of supply rules to prevent duplicate taxation.

VEHICLE 3: THE INSOLVENCY RESOLUTION RACE
World: An insolvency resolution professional has 180 days to resurrect
  a distressed manufacturing conglomerate under IBC, negotiating between
  financial creditors, operational vendors, and tax authorities.
