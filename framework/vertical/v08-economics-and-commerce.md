## PART A: SUBJECT INVARIANTS

### A1: What Must Be Taught

FOUNDATIONAL LAYER:
  Scarcity and opportunity cost: production possibility frontiers,
    rational choice theory, tradeoffs at the margin.
  Supply and demand: price equilibrium, elasticity of demand and supply,
    consumer and producer surplus, deadweight loss.
  Macroeconomic aggregates: GDP computation (expenditure, income,
    value-added approaches), nominal vs real GDP, deflators.
  Money and banking: money multiplier, fractional reserve banking,
    central banking instruments (repo, reverse repo, CRR, SLR).

PRACTICAL LAYER:
  Fiscal policy: revenue vs capital receipts/expenditure, fiscal deficit,
    primary deficit, FRBM targets, public debt sustainability.
  Monetary policy transmission: policy rates, inflation targeting
    frameworks (RBI MPC), yield curves, liquidity management.
  External sector: balance of payments (current and capital accounts),
    foreign exchange reserves, exchange rate regimes, tariff/non-tariff barriers.
  Market structures: perfect competition, monopolistic competition,
    oligopoly, and monopoly pricing strategies.

PROFESSIONAL / POLICY LAYER:
  Development economics: multidimensional poverty, human development index,
    inequality metrics (Gini coefficient, Lorenz curve), demographic dividend.
  International trade theory: comparative advantage, Heckscher-Ohlin model,
    WTO agreements, free trade agreements (FTAs).
  Economic Survey analysis: extracting macroeconomic trends, sectoral growth,
    capital formation data, and structural reform indicators.
  Policy evaluation: counterfactual analysis, unintended consequences,
    regulatory impact assessment.

### A2: Proof System

ALL economic data and models must feature:
  Exact statistical sources: RBI Database on Indian Economy, MoSPI,
    Economic Survey, Union Budget documents, World Bank, IMF.
  Mathematical formulas for every metric (elasticity, multiplier, deficit ratios).
  Visual economic graphs with properly labeled axes, equilibria, and shifts.
  Verification year and base year explicitly stated for all index numbers.

### A3: Interactive Element Types

Bazaar Simulation Workbench (bazaar-market.svg.js):
  Interactive marketplace showing buyers, sellers, price equilibrium,
  and deadweight loss shifts under taxation or price ceilings.

Macroeconomic Dashboard:
  Live visual metrics: Inflation (CPI/WPI), GDP growth, Repo Rate,
  Fiscal Deficit percentage, Forex reserves.

Policy Impact Flowchart:
  Shows policy intervention -> transmission channel -> market reaction -> outcome.
  Includes unintended feedback loops and friction delays.

Comparative Ledger / T-Account:
  Double-entry national accounts, central bank balance sheets,
  and balance of payments ledgers.

## PART B: CREATIVE VEHICLE PALETTE

VEHICLE 1: THE LOCAL BAZAAR SIMULATION
World: An ancient trading mandí in central India modernizes into an
  electronic trading hub, illustrating microeconomic price discovery,
  elasticity, and supply chain logistics through merchant transactions.

VEHICLE 2: THE CENTRAL BANK WAR ROOM
World: The Monetary Policy Committee meets during a global commodity
  shock, analyzing macroeconomic data, inflation forecasts, and bank
  liquidity to determine the policy repo rate.

VEHICLE 3: THE UNION BUDGET FORMULATION SPRINT
World: Ministry of Finance advisors and economic researchers assemble
  the annual Union Budget, balancing fiscal deficit constraints against
  urgent infrastructure and welfare spending demands.
