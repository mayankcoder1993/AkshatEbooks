# Exam Preparation Book Profile

Use for syllabus-led notes, practice books, revision guides and mock examinations.

## Required brief fields

Exam authority, exact exam name, subject, syllabus/version year, paper pattern, marks, duration, language, learner stage, target date and permitted materials.

## Source hierarchy

Use the official syllabus, exam notification, marking scheme, specimen papers and official past papers as authority. Record the applicable year because patterns change. Coaching material and memory-based papers are secondary and must be labelled accordingly.

## Content architecture

- Map every chapter and question set to a syllabus objective.
- Separate concept teaching, recall, worked examples, timed practice and revision.
- Show marks, expected time and difficulty where useful.
- Provide complete worked solutions and explain why distractors are wrong.
- Include a coverage matrix so heavily practised topics do not hide missing topics.
- Build cumulative revision and realistic mock papers only after foundation practice.

## Question integrity

Create original questions unless reuse rights are confirmed. Identify official past questions accurately; do not present invented questions as official. Keep answer keys and explanations synchronized with question versions.

## Claims and fairness

Never guarantee rank, score or selection. Avoid leaked, confidential or improperly obtained material. Use inclusive contexts and check that language difficulty does not accidentally test something outside the stated objective.
