# School Textbook Profile

Use for school subjects, workbooks and curriculum-aligned learning.

## Required brief fields

Country/state or board, curriculum document and year, grade, subject, term, classroom/home use, teaching language, assumed prior learning and accessibility needs.

## Source hierarchy

Use the current official curriculum, standards and prescribed terminology. Consult reliable subject references and age-appropriate pedagogy. Where boards differ, name the selected board instead of blending requirements silently.

## Chapter design

- Start with prior knowledge and a clear learning objective.
- Use age-appropriate explanation, examples and visuals.
- Include guided practice before independent exercises.
- Mix recall, understanding, application and reasoning.
- Provide formative checks and a chapter review.
- Keep answer keys or teacher guidance clearly separated from learner pages.
- Suggest safe, feasible activities with materials and supervision notes.

## Accuracy and inclusion

Check dates, units, maps, scientific diagrams, mathematical notation and vocabulary carefully. Avoid stereotypes and tokenism. Use contexts familiar to the intended learners while exposing them thoughtfully to wider contexts.

## Safeguarding

Activities must be physically and emotionally appropriate for the stated age. Mark adult supervision needs. Never request unnecessary personal disclosure from children. Escalate sensitive health, abuse or crisis topics for qualified review.
