# Core Editorial Standard

These rules apply to every Sarva Gyana Koshah Books title.

## Reader contract

- Define the exact reader before drafting.
- State what the reader will be able to understand or do.
- State prerequisites and exclusions.
- Build a deliberate sequence; do not assemble disconnected notes.
- Use plain language appropriate to the reader's age without speaking down to them.
- Define unfamiliar terms where they first become necessary.

## Source and research standard

- Treat transcripts, notes and AI output as leads rather than verified truth.
- Research beyond supplied material when doing so improves accuracy, context or teaching.
- Prefer primary and official sources; use reputable secondary sources for explanation or synthesis.
- Distinguish established fact, interpretation, analogy, opinion and advice.
- Keep a research ledger containing URL, publisher/author, access date, claim supported and one-line takeaway.
- Never copy a source's distinctive wording or structure without permission and attribution.
- Check image, quotation and question-bank rights before publication.

## Learning design

Every chapter should have a purpose, prerequisite, explanation, concrete example, active learner task, feedback or solution, summary and transition. Choose activities appropriate to the selected profile; code execution is not mandatory outside technical books.

Use progressive disclosure:

1. Familiar situation or question
2. Concrete example
3. Clear explanation
4. Name for the idea
5. Guided application
6. Independent check
7. Connection to the next idea

## Visual and accessibility standard

- Every visual must teach or orient, not merely decorate.
- Use meaningful alt text and short captions.
- Do not encode meaning through color alone.
- Preserve readable contrast and light-mode print quality.
- Tables require clear headers and should flatten sensibly on narrow screens.
- Interactive information must remain available in static PDF and Word outputs.

## Integrity and safety

- Never promise guaranteed marks, income, health outcomes or transformation.
- Label uncertainty and limits honestly.
- Do not fabricate quotations, statistics, credentials, case studies or citations.
- Protect personal and sensitive information found in transcripts.
- Escalate medical, legal, financial, safeguarding or crisis material to appropriate profile rules and qualified review.

## Publication gate

A title is not ready until its structured source, Web View, Book/PDF View, editable DOCX and offline HTML agree; its catalog status is truthful; its links and citations are checked; and its branding assets are approved.
