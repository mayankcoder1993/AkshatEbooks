## 0. AGENT MISSION BRIEF & IMAGE AUDIT PROTOCOL (READ FIRST)

> 🛑 **MANDATORY PRE-GENERATION AUDIT CRITERIA:**
> 1. **Character Hard Locks:**
>    - **Akshay (Apprentice Engineer):** 24-year-old North Indian male, lean athletic build, dark wavy hair. **Clean, natural forehead with ZERO tilak or markings**. Crisp **white handloom cotton kurta** with sleeves rolled up to mid-forearm, red sacred thread (*kalava*) on right wrist, light khaki trousers, dark brown leather Kolhapuri sandals. Carrying or working on a white aluminum laptop with a **distinct horizontal silver scratch across the top-left lid edge**. Spiral graph paper notebook and brass drafting pen.
>    - **Sameer Krishnamurthy (Principal Systems Architect):** 40-year-old South Indian male, serene poised presence, neatly trimmed salt-and-pepper beard, silver streaks at temples, thin round brass wireframe spectacles. Wearing a **peacock-indigo raw-silk kurta** with gold mandarin collar embroidery, ivory dhoti with gold woven border (*kasavu* edge). Subtle sandalwood mark on forehead. Always holding a **traditional faceted cutting chai glass in a raw brass wire holder**. Hands never touch a junior's keyboard.
>    - **Transit Operations Lead:** Mid-40s Indian male, heavyset, slate-gray Nehru jacket over light blue shirt, RFID transit lanyard, analog stainless steel wristwatch.
> 2. **Environment Hard Lock:** Solid **300-year-old carved Indian red and beige sandstone architecture** (Dravidian pillared halls, geometric jali screens, Burma teak tables) integrated seamlessly with **modern high-throughput hardware** (matte-black server consoles, ultrawide curved OLED displays, braided cables, brass task lamps).
> 3. **Screen & Document Authenticity (NO FOLK-ART IN TECHNOLOGY):**
>    - Screens, terminals, laptops, and printed documents must look like **authentic, sharp, photorealistic developer equipment**.
>    - Terminals show **real Node.js / Express CLI text and real stack traces** (`TypeError: Cannot read properties of undefined (reading 'trim')`).
>    - Postman interfaces show **real HTTP status badges (200 OK in emerald `#10B981`, 400 Bad Request in amber `#F59E0B`, 500 Crash in red `#E53935`)**, real JSON payloads, and real Chai BDD assertions.
>    - Printed sheets show **real printed monospace fonts and checklist boxes**.
>    - Absolutely NO decorative tribal patterns, folk squiggles, or pretend runes on screens or papers!
> 4. **Framing & Dialogue Ceiling:**
>    - **Strictly 16:9 full-bleed borderless.** No outer borders, no floral borders, no photo frames.
>    - **Dialogue Ceiling:** Keep the **top 25% to 30% of the canvas clean negative space** (stone arches, vaulted ceiling, ambient lamp pool, or sky) to allow dynamic SVG speech balloons.
>    - **NO baked-in speech bubbles or floating text in the raster artwork.** All dialogue lives in SVG overlays.
